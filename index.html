FAQ

Common Questions

Have a different question? Call or text (207) 713-1857.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. When a job requires a permit, it gets pulled and the work gets inspected.

How does a panel upgrade work, and will my power be off?

You get the quote first. After that, the permit gets pulled and the power shutoff gets coordinated with Central Maine Power. Expect your power to be off for part of the day while the panel is replaced. You'll know the timing ahead of time.

Do you handle generator hookups?

Yes, both hookups for portable generators and transfer switches for standby units. Send a photo of your panel and tell us which generator you have or want.

Do you do commercial work too?

Yes, commercial work as well as residential. Request a quote and choose Business as the property type, or call (207) 713-1857.

I have an electrical emergency. Can you help?

Call (207) 713-1857 right away instead of using the form. If you smell burning or see sparks and can safely reach the main breaker, turn it off. If there's smoke or fire, get out and call 911.
